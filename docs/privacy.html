Privacy

Privacy policy

The product is designed to analyze local files without uploading them to Qy.

What the software does

The CLI reads the files or feed documents you select, performs comparisons, and writes a report you choose. The software does not require a Qy account and does not send input files to a Qy server.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Paddle. Payment details are handled by the payment provider according to its privacy policy.

Support data

If you contact support, the message and any files you voluntarily attach are used to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, private keys, or production credentials.
